### Looking back to the two centuries of life science.

Today, the subjects of life science has expanded into diversified and intergrated disciplines. Dating back to two centuries ago, few of the well-acknowledged people in this world knew the secrets of life.  

Nowadays, most biologists are unable to clearly distinguish from each other, for the subdomains of biology has been so intergrated together. The classifications are quite varied. In the early 19th century, with Darwin's book *On the Origin of Species* coming to world, biology had entered a new era.

Mendelian genetics drived the modern evolutionary biology.

Also, genetics was formed as the re-discovery of Memdelian law. When entering the mid 20th century, molecular genetics appeared.

Biochemistry was started with researches in ensymes and metabolism in the late 19th century.

Cell biology was raised by Matthias Schleiden and Theodor Schwann. As the rapid development of electron microscopy and molecular biology, biologists developed modern cell biology.

Molecular biology was inspired by a series breaking discoveries, from DNA is the inheritery material in 1944, understanding of double-strand structure of DNA in 1953, and in 1958 the center theory. DNA as template, transcription generate RNA to guide protein production.
